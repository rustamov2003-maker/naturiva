The four foundations

  • Hygiene: sterilized tools, a disinfected workspace, gloves.
  • Preservation: anything with water needs a broad-spectrum preservative.
  • pH: match skin's acid mantle, about 4.5–5.5.
  • Patch test: inner arm, 24–48 hours, every new product.

How a formula is built

Every formula in the book groups ingredients into phases, each added at its own moment:

  • A Water or hydrosol phase
  • B Oil phase with the emulsifier
  • C Heat-sensitive actives, added at 38–40 °C
  • D Preservation, at about 35 °C
  • E pH adjustment, drop by drop

Three kinds of product

Emulsions (the creams) bring oil and water together with an emulsifier, both phases heated to 70–72 °C and combined under high shear. Gels and cleansers are water-based, built at room temperature with gums, polymers or mild surfactants. Anhydrous products (facial oils, butters, scrubs, bath salts) contain no water, so they need no preservative, only antioxidants and completely dry tools.

A sample from the book: Calming Chamomile Foam Cleanser

The book's first formula: a pH-balanced foaming cleanser for sensitive skin, mixed without heat or emulsifying. Yield about 186 g; target pH 4.5–5.5; shelf life 9 months.

CCranberry extract3.0
CBetaine2.0
CCaprylyl glycol1.5

DGeogard Ultra (or Leucidal Liquid, 3.6 g)1.8
DLavender (Lavandula angustifolia) oil0.3
pHCitric acid 10% solutionq.s.
  1. Dissolve Phase A, warming gently to 30–35 °C if needed.
  2. Combine the Phase B and C surfactants, extracts, silk, betaine and caprylyl glycol.
  3. Pour into Phase A slowly while stirring, to avoid foam.
  4. Premix polysorbate 80 with the lavender oil until clear, then add.
  5. Add the preservative and adjust the pH drop by drop.
  6. Bottle in a foaming pump and rest 24 hours.
Before you use it

Work to the hygiene protocol, and patch test on your inner arm for 24–48 hours before first use.

Skincare questions, answered

Natural Skincare

Why should facial oil go on damp skin?

On damp skin, the oil forms a micro-emulsion that spreads evenly, absorbs better and doesn't leave a greasy film.

How much essential oil is safe in face products?

No more than 1% in total, or 0.3 g in a 30 g product. Avoid phototoxic citrus oils in daytime face care.

Why is my body butter grainy?

Shea and cocoa butter formed large, unstable crystals as they cooled. Controlled cooling and longer whipping prevent it.

Why is copper peptide serum blue, and what if it turns green?

GHK-Cu is naturally sapphire blue. Green or brown means it may have oxidized and lost stability.

Why does an exfoliating toner need such a low pH?

Salicylic acid only exfoliates below pH 4.5, so the toner is set at 3.8–4.2. Lactic acid and Azeclair also work better in acidic conditions.

How long do homemade skincare products last?

From the book: cleanser, creams and toner 9 months; peptide serum and eye gel 6 months; facial oil 9–12 months; cleansing oil and shower gel 12 months; butters, scrubs and bath salts 6 months unopened, 3 months opened.

Why do the body scrubs contain Polysorbate 80?

It emulsifies the oils with water on the skin, so the scrub rinses off as a milky emulsion instead of leaving a greasy film.

Why are my bath salts fizzing or clumping in the jar?

They've been exposed to moisture. Make and store them completely dry.

How do I double cleanse with a cleansing oil?

Massage the oil onto dry skin with dry hands, add water so it turns milky, rinse, then follow with a mild foam cleanser.
