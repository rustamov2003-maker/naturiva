Knowledge / Soapmaking

Why is there a translucent ring in the middle of my soap?

Short answer

That's a partial gel: the center heated through gel phase but the edges didn't. It's cosmetic only; extend the cure.

Explanation

Uneven insulation or cooling lets the warm center gel while the cooler edges stay opaque. The bar is sound; it's a visual issue. Because ungelled soap cures more slowly, extend the cure to 6–8 weeks.

What to do

  • Insulate evenly if you want full gel, or chill evenly if you don't.
  • Commit to one approach per batch rather than something in between.

Source: The Art of Personal Soapmaking — The Gel Phase in Cold & Hot Process Soapmaking
