Knowledge / Candle Making

What temperatures should I use for coconut wax candles?

Short answer

Melt fully at about 70 °C, add fragrance at 55–60 °C, pour at 55–60 °C, and never exceed 85 °C.

Explanation

The book's reference card for coconut wax: container wax melts at 43–45 °C and mold wax at 57–63 °C. Melt completely at about 70 °C in a double boiler on low heat. Add solid dyes at 70–80 °C and liquid dyes at 55–60 °C. Warm containers to about 40 °C before pouring. Candle gel is different: it melts and pours at about 90–105 °C and must never exceed 110 °C.

What to do

  • Use a kitchen thermometer; don't judge temperature by eye.

Watch out for

  • Never let wax boil. Melt it only in a double boiler, never over an open flame. If wax catches fire, don't use water: smother it with baking soda.

Source: The Big Book of Sweet Dessert Candles — Coconut Wax · Decorative Container Candle
