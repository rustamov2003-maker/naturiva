Why do the body scrubs contain Polysorbate 80?

Short answer

It emulsifies the oils with water on the skin, so the scrub rinses off as a milky emulsion instead of leaving a greasy film.

Explanation

The scrubs use 4–5% Polysorbate 80. Melt the butters, stir in the Polysorbate, add liquid oils, chill to a frosting consistency, then fold in the exfoliants, extracts and essential oils. Use fresh, dry, finely ground coffee: used grounds bring in moisture and bacteria.

What to do

  • Keep water out of the jar; scrubs are anhydrous and have no preservative.

Watch out for

  • Red pepper (Capsicum) extract in the coffee scrub is potent: wear gloves, keep it away from eyes and broken skin, and wash your hands afterwards.

Source: The Art of Natural Skincare — Invigorating Coffee & Orange Body Scrub
