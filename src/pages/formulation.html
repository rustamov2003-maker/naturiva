The emulsion timeline

From the Barrier Repair Cream method. Each temperature exists to protect something.

  1. 70–72 °CHeat oil and water phases separately; hold the water phase about 5 minutes
  2. 70–72 °CPour oil into water in a thin stream; mix on high 3–4 minutes
  3. 38–40 °CAdd actives one at a time while cooling
  4. 35 °CAdd the preservative; mix 1–2 minutes; check the pH
  5. 25–28 °CStop stirring, pack, and rest 24 hours

The sterilization chain

  1. Clean in hot soapy water.
  2. Rinse with distilled or very hot water.
  3. Sterilize or disinfect: pressure cooker (15 min at 15 PSI), dry-heat oven (160 °C, 2+ hours), or 70% isopropyl alcohol and air-dry. Never towel-dry.
  4. Sanitize containers with alcohol just before filling.
